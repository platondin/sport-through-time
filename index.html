THE BASICS

WHAT IS
A SPORT?

Sport is more than a game. It is movement, skill, rules and challenge — a way for people to test themselves and connect with others.

Sports can be individual or team-based. They can be played for fun, health, education or serious competition.

01MOVEUse your body and energy.
Running, swimming, jumping and many other activities turn physical movement into structured competition.
02SKILLPractice turns effort into ability.
A sport usually rewards technique, timing, coordination, strategy or a combination of several skills.
03RULESRules make competition fair.
Rules define what players can do, how points are scored and how winners are decided.
04CHALLENGEPush your limits.
Competition gives people a reason to improve, overcome limits and measure progress.

WRESTLING

Wrestling is often described as one of the world's oldest sports. Evidence of wrestling appears thousands of years ago, and it was part of the ancient Olympic Games.

Ancient wrestling appears in art and historical records from several cultures. In the ancient Greek Olympic Games, wrestling was a major combat sport. It was simple in concept: use strength, balance and technique to bring an opponent down.

NO FINAL ANSWER

There is no permanent “youngest sport”. New sports can be invented at any time. E-sports, drone racing and hybrid games show how quickly sport can change.

There cannot be one permanent youngest sport because people continue inventing new competitions. A new sport can combine technology, physical movement, virtual environments, unusual equipment or completely new rules.

THE BIG IDEA

WHEN DID WE
START INVENTING?

Humans have created games and competitions throughout history. But the invention of many recognizable modern sports accelerated during the 19th and 20th centuries, when schools, clubs and organizations began creating standardized rules.

This period helped transform informal games into modern sports with written rules, official competitions, clubs, governing bodies and international events.
